This essay was written for my Game Analysis assignment for my application to FIEA. Within a tight schedule of two weeks, I explored and researched the sociological themes present within both the narrative and gameplay of Chants of Sennaar, reaching a greater understanding of the thought process that went into the design and implementation of the game.
